About us

A focused holding company.

EAM Real Estate Holdings of Bonita LLC is a legal entity established to hold and invest in real estate assets.

We operate as a holding company: we acquire, own and manage property for the long run rather than chasing short-term trends. Our base is in Naples, one of Florida's most established property markets.
